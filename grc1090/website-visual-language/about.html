About

Summit Shine Window Care started in 2021, when childhood friends Jake Mercer and Casey Holt got tired of watching their neighbors’ windows fog up with hard-water spots every spring runoff.

What began as two guys with buckets and squeegees is now Steamboat Springs’ go-to window washing crew, serving homeowners, vacation rentals, and small businesses across Routt County.

We grew up skiing these mountains, so we know what mountain living does to a window: hard water, mud-season splatter, and grime on second-story glass nobody wants to climb a ladder to reach.

We keep our crew small on purpose. That means more care on every pane, and a face you’ll recognize the next time we’re back.
