About Locus

Phones are a lifeline and a distraction at the same time. Camps, schools, and job sites need people reachable in an emergency, but endless scrolling pulls attention away from the kids, the students, or the work in front of them. Most places only have two choices: take the phones away, or hope for the best.

Locus is a third option. People keep their phones, but the apps that pull them away go quiet during the hours that matter. When the shift, class, or session ends, everything goes back to normal. No one is handing over their phone, and no one is left out of reach.

Locus started in Steamboat Springs, Colorado, in 2026. It is built by a small team of college students who grew up watching phones change how camps, classrooms, and workplaces feel.

We are working with our first pilot partners now, starting with schools. If your team is fighting the same battle with phones, we would love to talk.
