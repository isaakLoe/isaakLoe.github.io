Introduction

Summit Shine Window Care

Summit Shine Window Care is a locally owned, single-location window washing company in Steamboat Springs, Colorado, providing interior and exterior window cleaning, screen cleaning, gutter cleaning, and post-construction detailing for homeowners, vacation rentals, and commercial properties in Routt County.

Target audience

The site serves homeowners and property managers in a mountain resort town who want to protect the view they pay for: streak-free glass free of the hard-water spots left by snowmelt and irrigation. That includes vacation-rental owners who need spotless windows before the next guest arrives, contractors finishing a new build who need construction dust and stickers removed, and shop owners who want an inviting storefront on Lincoln Avenue.

Visitors come to quickly answer three questions: what's offered, what it costs, and whether this is a crew they can trust with ladders on their property. Their main tasks are checking the service menu and pricing, reading about the company's experience with mountain homes, and requesting a free quote that fits a tight guest turnover or a short clear window between snowstorms.

About

Summit Shine Window Care started in 2021, when childhood friends Jake Mercer and Casey Holt got tired of watching their neighbors' windows fog up with hard-water spots every spring runoff. What began as two guys with buckets and squeegees is now Steamboat Springs' go-to window washing crew, serving homeowners, vacation rentals, and small businesses across Routt County.

We grew up skiing these mountains, so we know what mountain living does to a window: hard water, mud-season splatter, and grime on second-story glass nobody wants to climb a ladder to reach. We keep our crew small on purpose — more care, and a face you'll recognize the next time we're back.
